**Result: 125 unique image assets, covering all 132 distinct image URLs found on the page (the 253 raw URL references include the srcset size variants of the same files), plus 8 YouTube videos. Every one is used on the new page (0 unused). Two partner videos had no thumbnail on the page, so their own YouTube thumbnails are used.**

Notes
- Images were converted to WebP in `assets/img/` (`-sm` = 480 px variant for mobile `srcset`). Award and "recognised by" logos were converted to white monochrome versions (`*.webp`) for the dark logo grid, and a colour copy (`*-color.webp`) is kept.
