An Expertly Executed Walkthrough for
Performing FUE Hair Transplantation
FUE hair transplantation is done using local anesthesia to minimize risks and downtime. It’s minimally invasive, leaving the donor area intact and allowing patients to have short hair with no visible scarring. Most patients can return to work the next day after the procedure.  The method uses a specialized punch device of different diameters (0.6mm to 1.0mm) to extract follicular units with the necessary glands, resulting in minimal trauma and better outcomes. The procedure is performed using high-powered loupes and specialized instruments, allowing the doctor to work with precision for natural, virtually undetectable results.
STEP-01
Preparing the Donor Area
When it comes to hair transplant services, our professional team at HTCM takes great care in the initial stage of FUE hair transplantation. This involves identifying the recipient areas and creating an individualized plan to achieve an authentically natural-looking hairline. By meticulously trimming the hair in the donor area, we ensure the smooth identification and removal of robust and healthy donor hairs, providing our patients with optimal outcomes.
STEP-02
REMOVING AND PREPARING HAIR FOLICLES
The donor area is prepared, and individual follicular units are removed by making a small circular incision around each unit and extracting it using a tweezers-like instrument. The extracted grafts are then inspected under high-powered stereo microscopes by trained technicians and prepared for transplantation. This meticulous and precise process helps ensure the best results for the patient
STEP-03
Placing the Follicular Units
The prepared follicular grafts are then inserted into the recipient area through small incisions that match the natural hair growth pattern. The grafts are placed one by one, taking into account the number of hairs in each follicle to maximize the naturalness, density, and coverage of the thinning areas. The success of the procedure is largely dependent on the experience and skill of the FUE physician.
STEP-04
FUE healing and Hair growth
The small incisions made during the extraction of the follicular units will heal completely in a few days. After three months, patients should start to see new hair growth, and after six months, there should be noticeable regrowth. At that point, both the native and transplanted hair will continue to grow naturally.
Our FUE hair transplant services are open to people of all ages, races, and hair types. The clinic has tools of varying sizes to cater to different hair calibers during the transplantation process. Post-operative care and follow-up support are included in the service, helping patients through their after-care journey.

FAQ
Am I the right candidate for Hair Transplant?
Consulting a professional at FUE Malaysia is crucial when considering hair transplantation. Specialists thoroughly examine your hair loss, scalp condition, medical history, and desired results to provide personalized recommendations. Factors such as hair loss extent, donor hair quality, and overall health determine candidacy. HTCM offers tailored advice and support throughout your hair restoration journey.
What are the advantages of FUE Hair Transplant Method?
Minimal invasiveness: FUE hair transplantation is a minimally invasive procedure with no linear scarring or stitching.
Quic
k recovery time: The recovery time after FUE hair transplantation is shorter compared to other hair transplant methods.
Natural-looking results: The individual follicular units used in FUE hair transplantation allow for a more natural-looking hairline and growth pattern.
Minimal pain and discomfort: The procedure is performed under local anesthesia, minimizing discomfort and minimizing risks associated with general anesthesia.
Versatility: FUE hair transplantation can be used to restore hair to various parts of the body, including the scalp, beard, eyebrows, and chest.
Short hair style: The donor area can be trimmed very short after the procedure without any noticeable linear scarring.
No linear scarring: Unlike other hair transplant methods, FUE hair transplantation does not leave a linear scar in the donor area.
High success rate: FUE hair transplantation has a high success rate and long-lasting results, with transplanted hair growing and being maintained for a lifetime.
Is the procedure painful?
You can rest assured that at FUE Malaysia, our procedures are carried out under local anesthesia, allowing you to experience minimal to no pain throughout the process. (FUE Malaysia, professional)
Is there age limit for Hair Transplant?
Age is one of the important factor for hair restoration, discussing with your hair restoration doctor is very vital for a younger patient because of the progressive hair loss. There is no limitation for an elderly men to choose hair transplant.
Can a Women do Hair Transplant?
Yes. Usually, the trend is that many women nowadays come for hair transplant also some of them also come to lower their hair line. And, being a woman is not a restriction to choose hair transplant.
After how long my transplanted hair will start growing?
Your hair after transplant will grow after three months. After transplantation, it may take much time to grow the hair completely.
